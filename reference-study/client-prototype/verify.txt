- paragraph: Reading a CoA
- heading "What each result means" [level=2]
- generic: Purity · HPLC
- paragraph: High-performance liquid chromatography measures the fraction of the sample that is the stated compound.
- generic: Identity · MS
- paragraph: Mass spectrometry confirms the molecular mass matches the stated compound and sequence.
- generic: Endotoxin · LAL
- paragraph: Limulus amebocyte lysate assay quantifies bacterial endotoxin, reported in EU per milligram.
- generic: Sterility
- paragraph: Culture-based testing verifies the absence of viable microbial growth in the sampled vials.
