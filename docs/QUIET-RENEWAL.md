# Quiet surfaces, living imagery

September 24, 2026. Riley asked to lean further toward the original Quiet modern
prototype, with most interface color carried by the supplied product labels.
Manrope display typography, warm paper, charcoal controls, modest corner radii,
and neutral documentation surfaces return. The client's original logo and all
20 supplied product renders remain intact. The catalog regains its split product
introduction. The homepage uses the new coastal scene; About uses the new water
study; Quality retains the earlier glass study. Earlier visual directions remain
available alongside the two new directions in the presentation.

The imagery expresses freshness and renewal through environment and light.
No healing, rejuvenation, or age-reversal efficacy claims were added.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original outputs were copied
unchanged into the project; existing image files were not overwritten.

### First light

Asset: `public/images/studies/morning.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium editorial environmental photograph for a quiet, refined research brand website. The feeling is a bright new morning, renewed energy, openness and life. An empty pale limestone coastal cove in clear early morning sunlight, soft blue water becoming translucent pale aqua at the shore, gently breaking white foam, creamy sun-warmed rock, a few delicate coastal wildflowers and fresh grasses at the foreground edge. Tiny restrained hints of lilac and warm butter-yellow flowers, not a green-dominated scene. Tangible photographic texture, subtle film grain, soft real atmosphere, natural color with more life than a desaturated architectural image. A generous sea horizon and graceful curved shoreline, asymmetrical composition, airy sky. No people, buildings, products, text, logo, medical imagery or surreal elements. Landscape 3:2 composition, beautiful also cropped to a square from the center-right. Sophisticated coastal editorial photography, not a stock travel postcard, no saturated sunset, no HDR. High quality.

### Light in motion

Asset: `public/images/studies/tidal.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Premium abstract nature editorial photograph for a refined, quiet research brand. A close overhead study of clear, shallow coastal water moving over pale warm limestone. Delicate intersecting sunlight caustics and gentle ripples, luminous pale blue with very subtle apricot reflected light along one edge, natural ivory highlights, a small smooth stone barely visible beneath the water. The image should feel alive, fresh, spacious and restorative through natural light and movement. Restrained real-world color, not grey, no teal-green cast. Minimal yet sensuous texture, photographic realism, fine film grain, imperfect organic patterns, sophisticated editorial art direction. Landscape 3:2 crop. No text, people, products, plants, pools, tiles, logos, medical symbols, glitter, artificial gradients or CGI. No health claims or literal rejuvenation illustration. The water surface itself is the subject.
